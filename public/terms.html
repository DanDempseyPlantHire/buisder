Buisder Pilot Terms

Version 2026-10-14

Pilot terms: These are product terms for beta testing and should receive legal review before public commercial launch.

Accounts

Users must provide accurate account information and keep login credentials secure. Job seekers must confirm they are at least 16. Employer users must be authorised to act for the business they represent.

Jobs and applications

Employers are responsible for the accuracy and legality of job listings. Job seekers are responsible for the accuracy of applications and profile information.

Safety

Fraud, harassment, discrimination, misleading job listings, impersonation and misuse of personal information are not permitted. Buisder may close listings or suspend accounts while reviewing safety concerns.

Recruitment decisions

Buisder provides a platform for matching and recruitment workflow. Employers remain responsible for recruitment decisions, employment terms and compliance with applicable employment requirements.

Beta service

Features may change during the pilot and availability is not guaranteed. Users should keep copies of information they need outside the service.
