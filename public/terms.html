2. Eligibility and accounts

Job seekers must confirm they are at least 16. Employer users must be authorised to act for the business they represent. Users must provide materially accurate information, keep login credentials secure and must not impersonate another person or organisation.

3. What Deas Jobs provides

Deas Jobs provides job discovery, transparent rule-based job ranking, application, messaging, interview, offer and hiring workflow tools. Deas Jobs is not the employer and does not make hiring decisions for employers.

4. Free initial launch

No fee is charged to job seekers or employers in the initial launch. Employer access tiers control product capacity only. If Deas Jobs later introduces paid services, pricing and applicable commercial terms will be published before payment functionality is enabled.

Employers are responsible for job-listing accuracy and legality, recruitment decisions, communications, employment terms and compliance with employment and equality law. Employers must not request unnecessary sensitive information or use Deas Jobs to discriminate unlawfully.

6. Job-seeker responsibilities

Job seekers are responsible for the accuracy of their profile and applications. Users should not upload third-party information unless entitled to do so.

7. Matching

The candidate feed uses preferences and job attributes to filter and rank jobs. Match information is a navigation aid, not a promise of suitability, interview or employment. It is not used to rank applicants for employers.

8. Messaging, safety and moderation

Fraud, scams, harassment, discriminatory content, unsafe listings, impersonation and other unlawful or abusive use are prohibited. Users can report messages/listings and block accounts. 10. Employer integrations

Employer API keys are confidential credentials. Employers are responsible for securing, rotating and revoking them and for ensuring imported job data is lawful and accurate.

11. Notifications and installed app

Users may install Deas Jobs as a web app and may choose to enable Web Push notifications. Device permissions remain under the user's control. Notification delivery cannot be guaranteed, so users should also check Deas Jobs for important recruitment information.

12. Privacy

The Privacy Notice explains how personal data is handled. The Young Users Privacy Guide explains additional safeguards for users aged 16–17.
