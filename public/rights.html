Your Data Protection Rights

This page explains how to exercise data-protection rights through Deas Jobs. It does not limit any rights available under law.

Access

You can download a structured export from Account & Privacy. You may also make a formal access request in the app.

Correction

You can edit much of your profile directly. For information you cannot correct yourself, submit a rectification request.

Deletion

Deas Jobs has self-service account deletion. If you need assistance or want a specific legal assessment, submit an erasure request.

Restriction, objection and portability

The in-app data-rights form supports these requests. Whether a particular right applies depends on the processing and legal basis involved.

Response process

Formal requests are logged in Deas Jobs' compliance workspace with a target response date. Deas Jobs may need to verify identity before disclosing or changing personal data.

Contact

Privacy contact: [PRIVACY CONTACT TO BE CONFIRMED].

Supervisory authority

If you are unhappy with how your personal data is handled, you can raise a complaint with the Data Protection Commission in Ireland or another competent data-protection supervisory authority.
