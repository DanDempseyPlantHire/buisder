Buisder Pilot Privacy Summary

Version 2026-10-14

Pilot notice: This is the privacy summary used for the Buisder beta. Before a public commercial launch it should be replaced or reviewed as a full privacy notice identifying the final legal operator, contact details and any additional processors.

What Buisder stores

Account details, profile information, job applications, interview and offer history, uploaded CV/profile images, business profile information, notifications, security records, privacy preferences and safety reports.

Why it is used

To operate the job matching and recruitment service, secure accounts, support employer verification, provide application workflows, prevent abuse and administer the service.

Who can see job-seeker information

Profiles are not intended to be publicly searchable. Employers receive candidate information through an application, subject to the candidate's privacy controls. Precise matching coordinates are not shown to employers.

Your controls

Users can change profile-sharing settings, export account data, block relevant users, submit safety reports and request permanent account deletion from Buisder.

Young users

Buisder currently requires job seekers to confirm they are at least 16. For profiles marked 16–17, direct email, CV and profile-photo sharing are disabled by default in this beta.

Data deletion

Self-service account deletion removes the account and associated profile data. Uploaded files are deleted from Buisder storage as part of that process. Some de-identified moderation records may be retained so safety decisions can be audited.
