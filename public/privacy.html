2. What Buisder collects

Buisder processes account and contact information, job-seeker profiles, availability and job preferences, approximate and optional precise location used for distance matching, CVs and profile images, employer/business information, job listings, applications, interview and offer history, notifications, security records, privacy preferences, safety/moderation reports, pilot feedback, first-party product analytics, and records created when you exercise data-protection rights or make a complaint.

3. Why Buisder uses it

The main purposes are to create and secure accounts, match job seekers with jobs, operate applications/interviews/offers, verify employers, send transactional messages, prevent fraud and abuse, moderate the service, measure whether the service works, administer a controlled pilot, and meet legal obligations.

4. Proposed legal bases

Buisder's v21 compliance register maps service delivery and recruitment workflows primarily to performance of a contract, security/moderation and first-party analytics to legitimate interests subject to balancing assessments, and legal compliance to legal obligation. These mappings are working positions for legal review before public launch. Buisder does not use consent as a catch-all legal basis.

5. Matching and ranking

The candidate feed uses rule-based filtering and a fit score. Current inputs include interests/category, availability, distance or location, pay preference, employment type and experience. Minimum-age eligibility is used as a filter. The score orders jobs for the job seeker; it does not make an employer's hiring decision and does not automatically reject candidates.

6. Who receives data

Employers receive candidate information after an application, subject to the candidate's privacy settings. For 16–17-year-old profiles, direct email, CV and profile-photo sharing is disabled by default. Buisder also uses service providers for hosting/security/storage and transactional email. The current implementation uses Cloudflare services and Resend; contractual role, transfer and subprocessor reviews remain part of the production compliance checklist.

7. International transfers

Before public launch, Buisder will document whether any personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area and the applicable transfer mechanism or safeguard. The production processor register will contain this assessment.

8. How long data is kept

Buisder applies data minimisation and provides self-service deletion. The final retention schedule for recruitment, analytics, moderation and compliance records is deliberately not invented in this draft; it is tracked as a legal-review item in Admin → Compliance. CVs/profile images are removed from Buisder storage as part of account deletion under the current implementation.

9. Young users

Buisder currently requires job seekers to confirm they are at least 16. Users aged 16–17 receive additional privacy defaults. Children have data-protection rights in their own right. See the Young Users Privacy Guide.

10. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Buisder provides data export and account deletion controls and a formal data-rights request form inside Account & Privacy. See Your Data Protection Rights.

11. Complaints

You can make a privacy or service complaint inside Buisder. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland or another competent supervisory authority.

12. Security and incidents

Buisder uses account verification, bot protection, password hashing, session controls, private file storage and administrative audit records. Personal-data incidents are recorded in the compliance workspace for risk assessment and any required regulatory/data-subject notification.

13. Cookies and device storage

See the Cookies & Device Storage Notice. Buisder does not currently add third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

14. Changes

Material changes to the Terms or Privacy Notice are versioned and existing signed-in users are asked to review and accept the current versions.
