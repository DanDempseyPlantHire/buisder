Young Users Privacy Guide

For Buisder users aged 16–17

Short version: Buisder uses your information to show you relevant jobs and let you apply. Your information belongs to you, and you can ask what Buisder holds, correct it, export it or ask for deletion.

What information do we use?

Your account details, profile, job preferences, applications and recruitment history. You can optionally upload a CV/photo and use location for distance matching.

What can employers see?

Employers see information through an application. For profiles marked 16–17, Buisder disables direct sharing of your email, CV and profile photo by default. Employers can still invite you to an interview and send an offer through Buisder.

How does job matching work?

Buisder filters out jobs that do not match selected requirements such as age eligibility and your chosen filters. It then gives jobs a fit score using interests, availability, distance/location, pay preference, work type and experience. The score only helps order your feed. It does not decide whether an employer hires you.

Do we sell your data?

Buisder is not designed to sell personal data to advertisers. The current product does not contain targeted advertising.

What choices do you have?

Inside Account & Privacy you can change sharing settings, export your data, make a data-protection request, report or block an account, and delete your Buisder account.

Growth and referral links

If you join through a Buisder referral, school/college partner or campaign link, Buisder may record that source to understand whether the service is reaching young people effectively. This information is not used to decide whether an employer should hire you and is not used to target third-party advertising.

Messages and safety

An employer can message you only after you have applied for one of its jobs. You can report a message or block an account. Buisder also uses behavioural safety flags for human review; these flags do not decide whether you get a job.

App notifications

You can choose whether Buisder sends push notifications to your phone or browser. You can switch them off at any time. Important application information also remains available inside Buisder.
