Cookies & Device Storage Notice

v21 cookie audit draft: the final classification should be confirmed before public launch.

Session cookie

Buisder uses a secure session cookie so that a signed-in user remains authenticated. This is intended to be strictly necessary for the service requested by the user.

Feed preference storage

Buisder currently uses browser local storage to remember job-feed filters selected by the user. The v21 compliance checklist requires a formal ePrivacy/cookie audit to confirm the production classification and whether any change to storage or consent handling is needed.

Cloudflare Turnstile

Buisder uses Cloudflare Turnstile for bot protection during authentication. Its production technical storage/signals and contractual privacy position should be confirmed as part of the processor and cookie audit.

Analytics

Buisder's product analytics are sent to Buisder's own backend after signed-in product events. The current product does not add Google Analytics, advertising pixels or targeted-advertising cookies.

Consent banner

If the production audit confirms that Buisder uses only storage that qualifies for an applicable strictly-necessary exemption, a consent banner may not be needed. If non-essential cookies or device storage are added later, consent controls must be implemented before they are used.
