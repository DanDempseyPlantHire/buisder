How Deas Jobs works

  1. Explore: Browse publicly listed jobs from verified employers.
  2. Join: Create a job seeker or employer account at no charge during the free launch.
  3. Connect: Job seekers apply and track updates; employers manage applicants and interviews.

Public listings are accessible without signing in. Applying to a job requires a candidate account.
