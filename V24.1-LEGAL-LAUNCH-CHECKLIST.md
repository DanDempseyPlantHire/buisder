# Buisder initial free-launch legal/admin checklist (Ireland)

This is an operational checklist, not legal or tax advice.

## Company and filings
- Confirm the legal entity that operates Buisder and show its legal/controller details in the app.
- If using an Irish limited company: maintain CRO registered-office/director/secretary records and file B1 annual returns on time.
- Register beneficial ownership with the RBO within the applicable deadline.
- Register the company for Corporation Tax with Revenue and file CT returns even if the launch produces no sales revenue.
- Keep proper books/records for company expenditure and transactions.
- Register PAYE only when director/employee remuneration requires it.
- No sales VAT is collected from users/employers in this free launch; separately review VAT obligations on taxable services received from suppliers outside Ireland.

## Recruitment-specific
- Obtain written clarification from the WRC on whether Buisder's matching/application/interview workflow requires an Employment Agency Licence. A free service is not automatically outside the licensing regime.
- Do not launch as an employment agency before any required licence is in place.

## Data protection and online service
- Populate controller/company/contact details in Admin -> Compliance.
- Finalise Privacy Notice, Terms, Young Users notice and rights/complaints process.
- Complete DPIA/LIA/processor/transfer/retention reviews.
- Complete safeguarding review for 16-17-year-old users.
- Confirm Digital Services Act applicability and moderation/notice-and-action duties.

## Free-launch commercial position
- No Stripe keys or webhook.
- No card checkout or subscription billing.
- No paid featured jobs.
- No cash/credit/points referral incentive.
- If payments are introduced later, treat that as a separate release with tax/VAT, consumer/commercial terms and privacy updates before activation.
